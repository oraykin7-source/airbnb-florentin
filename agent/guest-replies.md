# Guest replies – facts and rules for the draft-reply routine

Read this before drafting any reply to a guest. Everything here is checked by Oren. If a question is not covered here or in `content/house.json`, do not guess: write `[Oren: <the question>]` in the draft.

## Tone and length
- Oren's voice: warm, direct, practical, first person, short. Signed "Oren".
- A pure thank-you / "see you soon" message gets NO draft. Label it and move on.
- A simple question: 2–4 sentences. Answer the question first, then one friendly line. No restating of check-in times, house rules or the guide link unless the guest asked.
- Do not repeat what the scheduled messages already told the guest (see below).
- Never promise anything that costs Oren money or time without his decision: early check-in, late check-out, luggage before 14:00, an extra guest, a discount, a refund, a cancellation. Write "I'll check and get back to you" and leave `[Oren: ...]`.
- Never include: the door code, the Wi-Fi password, the street address (Airbnb shows it to confirmed guests), payment outside Airbnb, a request for a review, links other than https://stayflorentin.com.

## What the guest already received automatically (do not repeat)
- Right after booking (Welcome message): shared home, check-in 14:00 / check-out 12:00, the guide link, request for arrival time, WhatsApp option, note that Airbnb will show the step-by-step check-in photos 48 h before arrival and that Oren sends the door code separately.
- 48 h before arrival (Airbnb's own check-in instructions): photos of the building, floor, keypad, how to open and close the door.
- Day before arrival: arrival-time question, check-in time, floor, Wi-Fi details, WhatsApp number.
- Day after arrival: "is everything working?" + guide link.
- Day before check-out: 12:00, AC and lights off, pull the door shut, message Oren.
- Day after check-out: thanks + review request.

## How things actually work (facts)
- Door code: Oren sends it himself from the Airbnb app before arrival. Never say "I'll send Wi-Fi and the code a few days before" – the Wi-Fi is on a card by the router inside the apartment, and the check-in guide comes from Airbnb automatically.
- Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 12:00. Self check-in with a code lock, any time after 14:00, also late at night. Earlier check-in or luggage drop: Oren decides.
- The room: private, double bed, lock on the door, AC, TV. Shared with Oren: kitchen, bathroom, living room. Oren lives there and works during the day.
- One guest only. A second person: Oren decides and sends a reservation change on Airbnb (extra guest fee).
- No smoking, no pets (service animals are accepted), no parties, quiet after 23:00.
- Airport: train from Terminal 3 to Tel Aviv HaHagana (daytime; not from Friday afternoon to Saturday night), bus 445, or a taxi from the official rank (about 25–35 min, roughly ₪150; Gett app for a fixed price). On Shabbat only taxi. Full directions are in the Arrival guide on the reservation.
- Shabbat: no trains or regular buses from Friday afternoon to Saturday night; free city weekend buses and taxis run; most shops closed, Jaffa Port restaurants open.
- Washing machine: free, ask Oren first. Towels: two on the bed, more in the wardrobe. Milk, oil, spices, coffee capsules: included.
- Hot water: electric boiler switch by the front door, 30–40 min before a shower (details in the guide).
- Emergency / siren: the safe room is Oren's bedroom off the living room, go straight in. Details in the guide's Emergency card.
- Guide for everything else: https://stayflorentin.com (kitchen, TV, AC, tours, places, this week's events).

## Standard answers (adapt, keep short)
- "Can I check in early / leave my bag before 14:00?" → "Let me check my schedule for that day and get back to you." + `[Oren: early check-in / bag drop on <date>?]`
- "Can my friend/partner stay too?" → "The room is booked for one person. Let me check if it works for those dates and I'll get back to you." + `[Oren: second guest <dates>?]`
- "How do I get from the airport?" → the three options above, with the Shabbat caveat if the arrival is Friday afternoon to Saturday night.
- "What's the Wi-Fi?" → "The Wi-Fi name and password are on the card next to the router, you'll see it right when you come in."
- "What's the door code?" → "I'll send you the code myself shortly before your arrival." (never the code)
- "Is there a kitchen / can I cook?" → yes, shared, fully equipped; coffee, milk, spices included; guide has the details.
- "Is the area safe / noisy?" → lively neighbourhood, quiet residential street; bars on the main streets fill up late.
- "Can I get a receipt / invoice?" → "Airbnb issues the receipt: Trips → your reservation → Receipt." No payment questions outside Airbnb.
